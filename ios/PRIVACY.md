# TechShorts Privacy Policy

_Last updated: [date]_

TechShorts does not collect, store or share any personal data.

- **No accounts and no tracking.** The app has no sign-in, analytics, advertising or third-party SDKs.
- **Reading stays on your device.** Saved stories, settings and cached articles are stored only on your iPhone.
- **Summaries are made on your device.** When available, summaries are generated by Apple Intelligence on your iPhone. Article text is not sent to us or to any AI service.
- **Where stories come from.** The app downloads a list of the day's stories from our page on GitHub Pages (pranjaldoshi96.github.io). If that list is out of date, it downloads public RSS feeds and article pages directly from the publishers (for example techcrunch.com and theverge.com). GitHub and those sites receive the standard information any web request includes, such as your IP address, under their own privacy policies. Opening a story uses Safari's in-app browser.
- **Notifications** are scheduled locally on your device. No push server is involved.
