# Instagram setup — Techgist

Audience: Bangalore and Silicon Valley tech people. (Instagram is blocked in mainland China and Techgist isn't on the
China App Store, so Shenzhen is out for now; Hong Kong works if you want a China-adjacent audience later.)

## 1. Account type
Settings → Account type and tools → **Switch to professional account** → **Creator**
(Creator suits a media brand; switch to Business later if you run ads with a shop.)
- **Category:** `News & Media Website` (alternative: `App Page`)
- **Display category on profile:** On
- Then **Link to a Facebook Page** (create one named "Techgist" if you don't have it). This is required for automated
  posting through Meta's API later.

## 2. Profile
- **Username:** `techgistapp` (backups: `techgist.app`, `gettechgist`, `techgist.news`)
- **Name** (searchable, 64 chars): `Techgist · Tech news in 60 words`
- **Profile picture:** `instagram-profile.png` (this folder)
- **Bio** (150 chars):
  ```
  Today's tech, AI & startup news in 60 words a story 📱
  Ranked from 30+ sources, no ads, no tracking
  For builders in Bengaluru & Silicon Valley 👇
  ```
- **Link:** App Store link (use the campaign link from step 3 once made):
  `https://apps.apple.com/app/techgist-ai-startup-news/id6830218043`
  Link title: `Download free on iPhone`
- **Contact options:** email `pranjaldoshi96@gmail.com`
- **Pronouns / gender:** leave blank

## 3. Campaign link (so you can see installs from Instagram)
App Store Connect → **Apps → Techgist → App Analytics → Acquisition → Campaigns → Generate a Campaign Link**
- Campaign: `instagram_bio` → copy the link and use it as the bio link.
- Make one per platform later: `facebook`, `x`, `linkedin`, `reddit`, `youtube`.

## 4. Highlights (add after the first posts)
`How it works` · `Today` · `AI` · `Startups` · `Dev`

## 5. First grid (post these before promoting the account)
1. **Intro:** "Tech news in 60 words" — app screenshot + what it does
2. **How it works:** swipe tabs, ranking, on-device summaries, share as image
3. **Today in tech:** first daily carousel (cover + top 5 stories + download card)

## 6. Posting rhythm
Two posts a day reach both cities in their morning/evening (IST is 13.5 hours ahead of Pacific time until March):
- **8:30 AM IST** = 7:00 PM Pacific (previous day) — "Today in tech" carousel
- **8:30 PM IST** = 7:00 AM Pacific — "Overnight in Silicon Valley" carousel or a Reel

## 7. Hashtags (pick 8–12 per post, rotate)
Local: `#bengaluru #bangaloretech #bangalorestartups #siliconvalley #bayarea #sanfrancisco #techindia #startupindia`
Topic: `#technews #ai #artificialintelligence #startups #developers #programming #softwareengineering #productivity`
Brand: `#techgist`

## 8. Rules for posts
- Use Techgist's own branded cards; don't repost publishers' photos.
- Always credit the source in the card and caption ("via The Verge").
- No engagement-bait or follow-for-follow; it hurts reach.
